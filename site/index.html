  1. 2016

    A few pews apart

    Sunday School at St. Joseph's - two Grade 10 students who became friends first. On 29 October 2016, Pasindu asked her to be his.

  2. 2017

    Still a walk away

    We grew up together through school and sat our O/Ls side by side. Then life started moving, and distance came with it.

  3. 2018

    An ocean apart

    She left for Italy with her family. She would come back, stay a while, and leave again - and we learned to hold on through every goodbye.

  4. 2019

    Coming and going

    The last of the easy years. By December the distance had stopped being something we could simply wait out.

  5. 2020

    Years apart

    Then COVID. Borders shut, and the goodbyes stopped having return dates.

  6. 2021

    Still apart

    Another year of time zones, difficult days and countless conversations. Distance tested us. It never changed what we had.

  7. 2022

    0 km - and everything changed

    In December she came home. Pasindu spoke with her family, and with both families' blessing it stopped being only about the two of us. Two families were becoming one.

  8. 2023

    Between countries again

    Life kept taking us apart and bringing us back. By then we knew how to make the days in between count.

  9. 2024

    Home again in December

    She returned once more. After years of waiting, travelling and saying goodbye, the day we had always talked about was finally close.

  10. 2025

    16 January

    Almost nine years after Sunday School, we got engaged. Then one more goodbye - she left again, and came home that December.

  11. 2026

    The last year of waiting

    This time we both knew what we were getting ready for.

  12. 16 January 2027

    0 km - for good

    Back to the church where it began - the same place two Grade 10 students first became friends.
