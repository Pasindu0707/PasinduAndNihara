Ten years, two countries, one church

How we got here

  1. 2016 a few pews apart

    We met as children at Sunday school, in the same church we'll walk into on the sixteenth. It took a few more years and, on the 29th of October 2016, Pasindu finally asked.

  2. 2017 7,599 km apart

    In December, Nihara's family moved to Italy. A walk across town became a seven-thousand-kilometre phone call and a great deal of time-zone arithmetic. Neither of us treated it as an ending.

  3. 2018 — 2022 0 km, then 7,599 again

    She came home when she could. A bus ride. A rooftop in Colombo. A few weeks that went far too quickly, and then the airport again. We got very good at saying goodbye and very bad at meaning it.

  4. 2023 0 km

    In January, Pasindu went to meet her family properly. Very little felt uncertain after that.

  5. 16 January 2025 0 km

    He asked again — properly this time. She said yes, and then got on another plane, because that is how the last ten years have gone.

  6. 16 January 2027 0 km — for good

    Two years to the day. The same church. This time we don't leave separately.
