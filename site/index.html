Ten years, two countries, one church

How we got here

  1. 2016

    A few pews apart

    Sunday school, in the church we're marrying in. On 29 October, Pasindu finally asked.

  2. 2017

    7,599 km apart

    In December her family moved to Italy. A walk across town became a call across an ocean.

  3. 2018

    0 km, for a while

    Home for a season. We learned to make short weeks count.

  4. 2019

    0 km, for a while

    Another visit. Another airport. We stopped calling them goodbyes.

  5. 2022

    Still counting time zones

    Five years of it, and neither of us treated the distance as an ending.

  6. 2023

    0 km

    January. Pasindu met her family properly. Very little felt uncertain after that.

  7. 2024

    0 km

    A year of dressing up and staying out late, in the same country for once.

  8. 2025

    16 January

    He asked again — properly this time. She said yes, then got on another plane.

  9. 2026

    The last year apart

    One more year of airports. The last one.

  10. 16 January 2027

    0 km — for good

    Two years to the day. The same church. This time we don't leave separately.
